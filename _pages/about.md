I am a postdoctoral fellow in the ["Neuromodulation" research training group (RTG 2783)](https://uol.de/en/neuromodulation) at Carl von Ossietzky University of Oldenburg, Germany. I've studied medicine at Tehran University of Medical Sciences. My research interests broadly include utilizing neuroimaging tools (MRI/fMRI/dMRI) to investigate the brain's functional and structural changes underpinning the neurologic and psychiatric disorders. As a postdoctoral fellow, I work with large datasets and the data acquired within RTG, to investigate the network-based predictors of the non-invasive brain stimulation in Parkinson's disease and stroke.
